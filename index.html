03Crypto-agility

The first migration isn't the last.

Crypto-agility is the ability to swap algorithms across your stack — quickly, safely, repeatedly — as standards and threats change. The first PQC migration won't be the last. Algorithms get deprecated. New ones get standardized. Regulators add requirements. Organizations that come through these cycles maintain a current inventory, a working migration capability, and clear ownership.
